PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

Beast Pit ("the game") is published by TheSubMish.

What the game stores on your device. Your progress, settings, scores, stats and achievements are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts, and you never enter a name, email address or any other personal detail.

Usage data (you decide). The game can share anonymous usage data with us to help us fix crashes and balance the levels.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and wherever the game cannot tell where you are: the game asks first and sends nothing unless you choose "Yes, share".
  • Everywhere else: sharing is on by default, the first screen tells you, and you can turn it off there or later.
  • You can change this at any time in Options > Share usage data. Turning it off stops all sending. Erasing your save in Options makes the game ask again.

What is shared when it is on.

  • Game events: which levels you start and finish, how long they take, whether you died and roughly how far through the level, your score and floor in Endless, and which options you change (touch controls, game speed, shake).
  • Performance and crash information: frame rate, and crash reports with the level you were on and the game version.
  • Information the analytics service collects automatically: a random app-installation identifier (it is not your name and is not linked to you), device model, Android version, app version, language, an approximate region derived from your network address (the address itself is not kept by Analytics), and how the game was installed (for example from which Google Play listing, through Google Play's install referrer).

What the game does not do. It has no advertising and does not use your advertising ID. It does not sell your data, build profiles to target you, or use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Who processes it. The data is processed for us by Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics), under Google's terms: firebase.google.com/terms and policies.google.com/privacy. It is not shared with anyone else. Event-level data is kept for at most 14 months, and aggregated reports can be kept longer.

Permissions. The game uses internet access to send the data above (only when you allow it) and network state to know when it can. It needs no other permissions.

Your choices and rights. Turn sharing off in Options at any time. Because the data is not linked to your name, we cannot look up "your" data; if you want to ask a question or make a request about data protection, write to the address below and we will do what we can. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device and resets the installation identifier.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and the usage-data prompt is not meant for them.

Changes. If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the game asks again when needed.
